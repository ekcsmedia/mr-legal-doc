Introduction

Mr Legal (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the mobile application Mr Legal - Advocate (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you use the App.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile information: Advocate details required to deliver bookings and services.
  • Device & usage data: Device type, OS version, crash logs and analytics.
  • Location data: Real-time and background location when advocates go online.

Background Location Usage

The App collects location data in the background only when an advocate switches to online mode. This enables nearby booking discovery, navigation to the client’s location, real-time tracking, and accurate estimated arrival time (ETA) for customers.

Background location collection automatically stops when the advocate switches offline, logs out, or disables location permissions. We do not collect background location when the advocate is offline.

Permissions the App requests

  • Foreground & Background Location: Required for accepting nearby bookings, navigation, and real-time tracking during active service.
  • Internet access: Required to communicate with servers and deliver app features.

You can revoke permissions anytime from device settings.

How we use information

  • Provide booking, navigation, and tracking features
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Ensure service safety and dispute resolution
  • Send notifications related to bookings

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Location is shared only with customers during active booking sessions.

Data retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services and meet legal obligations.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect user information.

User control

  • Switch offline to stop tracking
  • Disable permissions anytime
  • Request data deletion via support email

Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13.
